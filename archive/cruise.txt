All Posts Eat Travel Relax Search Our First Cruise John & Carrie Feb 2, 2019 2 min read Yep, it was our first... we got the pleasure of spending our 13th anniversary on the beautiful Vision of the Seas, one of Royal Caribbean's smaller ships. This was our first cruise so we were not really sure what to expect, but we had the time of our lives. We left out of New Orleans and had three ports on the schedule, one in Nassau Bahamas, then Key West Florida, and along the way we stopped at Royal Caribbean's private island, Cocoa Cay. Nassau was okay but to really see the beauty of the island you have to get away from the port and all the markets. Understand it is a tourist town and everyone plays off making as much as they can from us that are traveling. One of the best things we saw in the Bahamas as we toured around was the Queen's Staircase seen below The Queen's Staircase, commonly referred to as the 66 steps, is a major landmark that is located in the Fort Fincastle Historic Complex in Nassau. It was hewn out of solid limestone rock by slaves between 1793 and 1794 and it is said that it provided a direct route from Fort Fincastle to Nassau City. These steps were later named in honor of Queen Victoria, who reigned in Britain for 64 years from 1837 to 1901. Key West was really a site that both me and my wife would like to visit again. We had fun walking around town and seeing some of the sites and even got to have lunch at the famous Sloppy Joes Bar... It is definitely a place we want to visit again when we have more time to explore. Sloppy Joes Bar Last but certainly not least was our favorite stop on the trip and this was the fabulous Cocoa Cay island, which is Royal Caribbean's private island. We spent the day being lazy on the beach bed and did some snorkeling. The water was so clear, it was just great. We had our lunch delivered to our cabana bed and never had to lift a finger until we were ready to leave, the problem is that we didn't want to leave. It is hard to be pulled away when your view looks like this... Cocoa Cay Of course this is only the time that we spend off the boat, the actual cruise was one of the most relaxing vacations that we have ever been on. The food was amazing and what made it even more special was the first class service that we got from the staff. Taffereal and Lucy, we will never forget how you made our time special. We simply cannot wait for our next cruise, which we are scheduled to be on soon, so look for more pictures from our adventures! Until next time, we hope your adventures are as fun as ours, God Bless.
